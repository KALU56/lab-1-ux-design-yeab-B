# Comparison Between the Current App and the Yeab-B Implementation

When comparing the current app with the Yeab-B implementation, I noticed some differences in the onboarding flow and overall user experience (UX).

1. **Loading Page:** When users open the current app, they first see a loading page.

2. **Language and Country Selection:** In the current app, users can select their preferred language and country at the beginning of the process. This makes the experience more personalized and accessible from the start.

3. **Onboarding Page:** After selecting their language and country, users proceed directly to the onboarding process.

4. **Direct Access to the App:** Once users complete onboarding, they are redirected directly to the main app without having to go through separate Sign Up or Sign In pages.

**Conclusion and UX Recommendation:**

The current app provides a more user-friendly and straightforward experience than the Yeab-B implementation. Its flow is simple and convenient: users select their language and country, complete the onboarding process, and then access the app directly.

This reduces unnecessary steps, makes the app easier to use, and allows users to get started quickly without additional registration barriers.
